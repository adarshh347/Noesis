# The Philosophy Behind Weltanschauung

## Core Principles

### 1. Philosophy Beyond Text
Traditional philosophy ends at the text. We move beyond:
- **Visual Interfaces**: Interactive concept maps, graphs, and flows
- **Dynamic Structures**: Ideas that grow and evolve with new thoughts
- **Multi-dimensional Representation**: Not just words, but relationships, patterns, and applications

### 2. Pragmatic Philosophy
Philosophy should contribute to human culture, not just win debates:
- **Pragmatic Applications**: Every insight connects to practical uses
- **Cultural Contribution**: Ideas that enhance human experience
- **Exponential Extraction**: Using AI to find deeper patterns and connections

### 3. Volatile, Growing Structures
Your thoughts are not static:
- **Organic Growth**: New notes connect to existing ideas
- **Evolving Visualizations**: Representations that change as understanding deepens
- **Fluid Boundaries**: Concepts that merge, split, and transform

## How It Works

### The Flow

1. **Capture**: You write your philosophical thoughts (raw, unfiltered)
2. **Extract**: AI analyzes and extracts:
   - Core themes
   - Key concepts
   - Arguments and positions
   - Connections to philosophical traditions
   - Pragmatic applications
   - Visual metaphors
3. **Visualize**: Multiple representations:
   - Concept networks (interactive graphs)
   - Theme distributions (charts)
   - Pragmatic flows (theme → application mappings)
4. **Evolve**: As you add more notes:
   - Connections emerge between ideas
   - Patterns become visible
   - The system learns your philosophical style

### The Interface

The UI is designed to be:
- **Beautiful**: Dark, thoughtful aesthetic that doesn't distract
- **Interactive**: Click, explore, discover connections
- **Meaningful**: Every visualization tells a story
- **Pragmatic**: Always showing how ideas can be applied
